# Math 3208 Review

A generated practice question bank for **Mathematics 3208** (Newfoundland and Labrador), built on the same chassis as the Math 2201, 2200, 3200, and 3201 Review apps.

**Live app:** https://cdli-nlschools.github.io/Math3208Review/

## What it does

- Draws fresh practice questions by unit or by curriculum indicator, with a level mix following the published 2026-2027 tables of specifications (about 20% Level 1, 60% Level 2, 20% Level 3 in both the mid-course and end-of-course scopes).
- Mid-course preset: Units 1 to 4 (Pre-Calculus; Limits and Continuity; Rational Functions; Derivatives). Final preset: Units 5 to 8 (Applications of Derivatives; Calculus of Trigonometry; Calculus of Exponential, Logarithmic, and Hyperbolic Trigonometric Functions; Antidifferentiation and Integration).
- Three ways to work: practice online with instant checking, print a worksheet (choices removed, working space provided), or print a practice exam built to the tables of specifications (27 selected-response marks and 23 constructed-response marks, with a separate teacher key).
- Every question carries a full worked solution.

## Sources and originality

Items are generated from templates written for this bank, informed by the Mathematics 3208 Curriculum Guide, the provincial tables of specifications, a type-level harvest of classroom practice sets, and the Memorial University Calculus Placement Test sample (the CPT is written for Math 3208 graduates). No question text, scenario, or values from any source are reproduced.

## Status
